# AI Assistance And Responsibility

Lou Thomas originated and directed this personal research program. AI systems performed substantial parts of the mathematical development, numerical coding, drafting, explanation, and automated checking. The work should not be described as mathematics independently derived or verified by a human researcher merely because multiple AI systems agreed.

This release adds organization, source recovery, privacy processing, code review, and selected reproduction checks. Those activities improve inspectability; they do not settle scientific novelty, correctness of every derivation, or observational validity.

Please distinguish original research hypotheses, established methods used by the code, computational checks, and speculative interpretation. Existing references are retained as supplied; the complete bibliography has not been independently reverified in this packaging pass.

AI systems are tools, not named scholarly coauthors. Questions and corrections should identify the exact document, equation, file, or test involved.
