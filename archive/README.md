# Historical Archive

Distinct earlier drafts, incomplete runs, late-universe branches, and legacy scripts are preserved here. Byte-identical duplicates were collapsed into one selected copy; `docs/SOURCE_MAP.csv` maps the original references to their public locations.

Executable legacy files use `.py.txt` or `.ps1.txt` extensions. They are source documents for review, not supported entry points. Some depend on unavailable inputs, download third-party data, or write to old workspace locations. Local paths are redacted. Do not rename and run them without adapting and reviewing their input/output contract.

Historical claim language is not silently rewritten. The release's research overview states the current evidence boundary. The earliest A2 RUN01 remains an incomplete experiment, not a substitute for the later sealed production record. The late-universe C-series material is not A2 evidence.

`RSI_v1` and `RSI_v2` are retained as separate conceptual branches: their contents differ enough that the filename alone cannot establish a normal revision chain.
